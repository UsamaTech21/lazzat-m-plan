3 SEO & LOCAL SEARCH DOMINATION
3.1 — Google Business Profile Optimization
  • Categories: Primary = "Pakistani Restaurant" | Secondary = "Indian Restaurant", "Grill Restaurant", "Halal Restaurant"
  • Description: Include keywords naturally — "Lazzat is Brampton's halal grill restaurant, serving tandoori, BBQ, and fusion dishes in a welcoming multiracial atmosphere..."
  • Photos: 50+ at launch — Food (30), Interior (10), Exterior (5), Team (5). Add new photos weekly.
  • Google Posts: Post every week — promotions, events, new menu items. Appear directly in search results.
  • Q&A: Pre-populate 8–10 Q&As before launch — "Is everything halal?", "Do you take reservations?", "Is there parking?", "What's the price range?"
  • Reviews: Target 25 reviews in first 30 days. Respond to every single one.
3.2 — Website SEO Priorities
  • Page titles: "Lazzat — Halal Grill Restaurant Brampton" on every page
  • Local schema markup: Restaurant type, hours, menu, location structured data
  • Menu must be an HTML page (not just PDF) so Google can index individual dishes
  • Blog from Month 2: "Best Halal Restaurants in Brampton", "Lazzat's Tandoori Grill Story" — long-tail keywords competitors ignore completely
  • Mobile speed: under 2.5 seconds. Test with Google PageSpeed Insights.
  • NAP consistency: identical Name, Address, Phone across every listing

5 RUSH & CROWD MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
No marketing plan succeeds if the restaurant can't manage the demand it generates. A guest who waits 45 minutes without communication never comes back and leaves a 2-star review. A guest who waits 30 minutes with a drink in hand and a text update becomes a loyal customer.
5.1 — Reservation System Setup
  • Platform: OpenTable or Yelp Guest Manager — both offer waitlist + table management + discovery
  • Enable online booking from: Google Business Profile, Instagram Bio link, Facebook page, and website
  • Friday and Saturday: reservations required for groups of 4+
  • No-show protection: credit card required for groups of 6+ on busy nights
  • Automated reminders: confirmation immediately + 24hr reminder + day-of reminder 3 hours before
6 CUSTOMER RETENTION ECOSYSTEM
6.1 — Digital Loyalty Program
No direct competitor in Brampton's South Asian restaurant segment has a formal digital loyalty program. This is Lazzat's first-mover opportunity.
  • Tool: Stamp Me, TastyRewards, or Square Loyalty (integrated with POS)
  • Mechanic: Every $10 spent = 1 point. 100 points = $10 off. Simple and transparent.
  • Bonus points: Double on birthdays, first visit, and special events
  • Enrolment: QR at table, link in WhatsApp, Instagram bio. Collect: name, email, phone, birthday.
  • Nudges: Automated "You're 20 points away from your reward!" emails
  • VIP Tier: $500+ lifetime spend = "Lazzat Insider" — priority reservation, chef's welcome, first access to new menu items

2 SENSITIVE DATA SHARING, COMMUNICATION & BACKUP
2.1 — Interdepartmental Communication & Data Sharing
To ensure seamless coordination and protect sensitive brand assets, all internal communication and real-time data sharing must be centralized.
  • Platform: Slack
  • Use Case: Creative approvals, campaign coordination, daily updates, and quick data sharing between Marketing, Creative, and Management teams.
  • Security: All sensitive marketing logins, strategy documents, and performance data are shared within private, encrypted Slack channels.
2.2 — Centralized Backup & Asset Storage
  • Platform: Google Drive
  • Use Case: Long-term storage for all raw content, finalized creatives, marketing reports, and legal documents.
  • Access Control: Highly restricted "Selected People Only" access. Only Department Heads and Team Leads have full editing rights; others are granted "View Only" or specific folder access as needed.
  • Structure: Organized by Year > Month > Campaign > Deliverable Type.
