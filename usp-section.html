BRAND FOUNDATION
1.1 — Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
Core USP: "Brampton's First Everyone's Table — Grill Culture Without Borders"

Lazzat enters a market where every competitor speaks exclusively to the South Asian diaspora. Brampton is home to South Asian, Jamaican-Canadian, Filipino, Arab, and West African communities living side by side — yet no restaurant has claimed multiracial Brampton as its identity. Lazzat owns this gap.

  • THE MULTIRACIAL TABLE — Brampton's gathering place for mixed friend groups, couples, and families where every person finds something they crave.
✦ Tagline: "Where Every Craving Has a Home" ✦
