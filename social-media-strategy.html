Phase 1 Strategy: Local Audience Anchoring
This initial budget is designed to be maintained until Lazzat reaches a critical mass of 5,000+ local followers on Instagram. By utilizing targeted paid ads in this phase, we ensure the platforms (Instagram & TikTok) identify and "lock in" our core Brampton audience.

Once this local foundation is established, we can effectively scale organic content—including creative production from Pakistan. Because the platforms distribute content to users similar to our existing engaged followers, this early investment ensures that our global content is always served to the right local customers.
