Grand Opening Activation

The Lazzat Ignition

A 30-Day Tactical Blueprint to Dominate the Brampton Grill Scene
"Where Every Craving Has a Home"
01

Campaign Overview

This isn't a generic launch—it's a 30-day "Algorithm Training" sprint designed to anchor Lazzat as the premier multiracial halal grill in the Brampton psyche.

🎯
Objective
Establish Lazzat as Brampton's premier multiracial halal grill destination—where authentic tawa craft meets "Everyone's Table"—targeting 500+ guests in Month 1.
💰
Budget
$2,200 Monthly Spend: 50% Paid Social, 25% Influencers, 25% Content.
📊
KPIs
1,500+ IG Followers, 100+ Google Reviews (4.5+ ★), 20% Repeat Customer Rate.
02

Pre-Launch Timeline

The foundation must be locked before the first sizzle hits the tawa.

Days -14 to -10
Digital Foundation
  • Infrastructure: GBP claimed with 50+ hero shots; website load time < 2.5s; HTML menu (no PDFs).
  • Reviews & WiFi: Table QR codes printed; WiFi portal email capture live; staff verbal ask script rehearsed.
  • Delivery: UberEats/Skip/DoorDash accounts live with professional menu photography.
Days -10 to -3
Content & Teaser Sprint
  • Hero Grid: Post 9 high-res images to IG (dark, moody, fire-lit) to create a cohesive brand hub.
  • TikTok Countdown: Daily "Sizzle ASMR" and "Founders Story" videos to build local anticipation.
  • Influencer Outreach: Confirm 5-10 local micro-influencers for Grand Opening Week coverage.
Final 72 Hours
Ignition Launch
  • Staff Drill: Final training on 'Dramatic Tawa Presentation' and review collection.
  • Ad Launch: Meta ads go live targeting 5km radius with "Opening This Week" creative.
  • Influencer Preview: Private soft-tasting for creators to ensure Day 1-2 content blitz.
03

Creative Requirements

Social Hub
Instagram Grid

9 Hero Posts minimum. Aesthetic: Dark, moody, fire-lit. Focus on the char, the steam, and the shared plate.

Discovery
TikTok/Reels

15-20 Clips. ASMR sizzle, kitchen hustle, and authentic customer reactions. Raw over polished.

Conversion
Meta Ad Assets

6 Variations. Video-first hooks: "Brampton's New Favorite," "halal grilled perfection."

04

The Launch Month (Week-by-Week)

Week 1: The Explosion
  • Day 1: Pack the house via "First 100" offer. Focus on generating 20+ reviews immediately.
  • Stories Blitz: 10+ Stories/day resharing guest UGC and showing kitchen energy.
  • Weekend Push: Increase ad spend Friday-Sunday to capture maximum local foot traffic.
Week 2-3: Algorithm Training
  • Analyze: Identify which dish/post got most traction; double down on that content style.
  • Retargeting: Serve ads to people who engaged Week 1 but didn't visit.
  • Community: Start the "Meet the Chef" series to humanize the brand.
Week 4: Retention Lock
  • Loyalty Blast: Announce digital loyalty rewards to Week 1-2 visitors via WhatsApp.
  • Referral Program: Launch "Bring a Friend" offer to activate existing fans.
  • Month 2 Prep: Review analytics and lock in content calendar based on Month 1 wins.
"Where Every Craving Has a Home"

Everything in this launch plan serves the fundamental promise: theatrical food, genuine hospitality, and a place where Brampton belongs.
